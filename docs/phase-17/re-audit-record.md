# Phase 17 Re-Audit Record

Every phase was re-audited during Phase 17. Each phase's own documents
originally carried the resulting record inline, which put audit bookkeeping
in the middle of the method and evidence. On 2026-09-19 those sections were
moved here verbatim and each source document was left with a pointer.

**Nothing in this file has been edited in the move.** Section headings and
bodies appear exactly as they were written; only their location changed. A
phase document therefore still names the audit outcome that applies to it,
and the full text sits here in one place.

Audit-related sections that carry substance rather than bookkeeping were
deliberately **not** moved, and remain where they were written: the
sampling-bias correction in `docs/phase-7/eda-results.md`, the audit-added
items in `docs/phase-15/phase-15-checklist.md`, the per-phase re-audit list
in `docs/phase-17/phase-17-checklist.md`, and the `Phase 17 Final Audit`
reconciliations in `docs/phase-0/curriculum-mapping.md` and
`docs/phase-1/requirements-traceability.md`.

One substantive finding did travel with its parent: the zero-denominator
resolution in `docs/phase-6/integration-results.md` is a *subsection* of
that phase's re-audit record rather than a separate section, so it moved
with it instead of being split out. It is indexed below.
