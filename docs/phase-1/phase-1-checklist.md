# Phase 1 — Business Understanding & Planning

## Purpose

Phase 1 frames the project: it defines the business problem, the analytical
questions and hypotheses, the stakeholders, the KPIs, the scope and the
requirement traceability that the later phases answer.

## What the phase delivered

| Area | Delivered |
|---|---|
| Business understanding | Business context, problem, primary and secondary objectives, the inventory relationship and the expected outcome ([`business-problem.md`](business-problem.md)) |
| Stakeholders | Primary, secondary and technical stakeholders, their information requirements and their decisions ([`stakeholder-analysis.md`](stakeholder-analysis.md)) |
| Analytical questions | Primary SMART question and supporting questions ([`analytical-questions.md`](analytical-questions.md)) |
| Hypotheses | H1–H8 with verdicts and evidence pointers ([`hypothesis-register.md`](hypothesis-register.md)) |
| KPIs | Historical and forecast demand, RMSE, MAPE, forecast bias, demand volatility, promotion comparison and inventory indicators ([`kpi-definitions.md`](kpi-definitions.md)) |
| Requirements | Business requirements and their traceability ([`business-requirements.md`](business-requirements.md), [`requirements-traceability.md`](requirements-traceability.md)) |
| Scope and assumptions | In-scope and out-of-scope activities, assumptions, constraints and scope-change handling ([`scope-and-assumptions.md`](scope-and-assumptions.md)) |
| Decisions | Decisions D001–D008 with their reasoning ([`decision-log.md`](decision-log.md)) |

## Scope at a glance

- **In scope:** historical demand analysis, promotion and price analysis,
  time-series forecasting with ARIMA and baselines, forecast evaluation with
  RMSE and MAPE, inventory-oriented scenario insights, visualization, an
  application and full documentation.
- **Out of scope:** production infrastructure, real-time integration, causal
  inference and holiday analysis (no holiday field exists in the dataset).

## Course coverage

Phase 1 carries the **Ask** stage of the six-stage lifecycle — SMART questions,
stakeholder analysis, problem types, hypothesis thinking and the data-driven
decision context. The Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share and Act stages are
delivered by the later phases.
